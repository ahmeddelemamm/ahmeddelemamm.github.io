About

Good data means nothing if no one understands it.

I'm a Data Analyst focused on turning data into clear and actionable insights through structured analysis and thoughtful dashboard design.

I help teams and clients better understand their data by focusing not only on the analysis itself, but also on how information is presented. I carefully structure KPIs and visuals to make dashboards clear, intuitive, and easy to understand, while adapting the design to a company's visual identity and paying attention to details that others may overlook.

I have completed practical projects using Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX, SQL, and Python, working with data across areas such as Sales, Retail, and Supply Chain. I'm always looking for opportunities to work on real-world Data projects, collaborate with others, and continue developing my skills.

If you have a Data-related project or an opportunity to collaborate, feel free to reach out.
